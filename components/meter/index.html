Meter

Show proportions faithfully, including the part that remains unresolved.

Basic usage

HTML
<div class="meter meter-lg" aria-hidden="true">
  <span class="meter-part tone-ok" style="--dd-part:4.15%"></span>
  <span class="meter-part is-open" style="--dd-part:95.85%"></span>
</div>
<ul class="legend">
  <li><span class="chip tone-ok">Resolved</span><span class="meter-legend-count">35 statements</span><span class="meter-legend-description">Fully determined SQL</span></li>
  <li><span class="chip chip-ghost">Unresolved</span><span class="meter-legend-count">809 statements</span><span class="meter-legend-description">Dependencies, analysis limits, or unexamined calls</span></li>
</ul>

Classes & attributes

Class / attributePurpose
.meter / .meter-lgThe standard bar, or a taller version.
.meter-partSet the percentage with --dd-part. Parts should total 100%.
.is-openHatching distinguishes an unresolved part.
.legendThe name, count, and explanation of every part.

Usage notes

The legend must contain all the information. Do not enlarge small categories to make them clickable. Print hides the bar and retains the legend.

Examples follow your system color scheme. Use ◐ in the header to try automatic, light, and dark themes.
